Frequently Asked HR Questions

Tell Me About Yourself

Introduce your education, skills, projects and career goals briefly.

What Are Your Strengths?

Highlight qualities that relate to the internship or job role.

Why Should We Hire You?

Explain how your skills and enthusiasm match the company's needs.

Where Do You See Yourself in 5 Years?

Show ambition, learning mindset and professional growth plans.

Interview Success Tips

  • Research the company before attending the interview.
  • Prepare a strong self-introduction.
  • Maintain eye contact and confidence.
  • Keep your resume handy and know every detail in it.
  • Practice common HR and technical questions.
  • Ask thoughtful questions at the end of the interview.
  • Dress professionally and arrive on time.
